# Roadmap

## Planned Specs

### 001 — Core todo list  [status: planned]

- **Description:** A single-user todo list where a person can create, view, edit, complete and delete todo items, with the list persisted between sessions.
- **Outcome:** A person can manage their own todo items end to end and find them again the next time they open the app.
- **Scope (in):** Create, edit, complete (and un-complete) and delete a todo item; list all items with their completion state; persist items between sessions; automated tests for each behavior.
- **Scope (out):** Accounts and sign-in, sharing or multi-user collaboration, due dates, reminders, filtering and search, third-party integrations.
- **Depends on:** none
- **Governed by:** Scope Boundaries, Spec-Driven Development and Quality Gate in the constitution.
